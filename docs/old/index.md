**Welcome to the Nazarbayev University High Performance Computing (NU HPC) Wiki!**

We are delighted to have you here as part of our growing HPC community. This Wiki is designed to be a comprehensive guide to high performance computing at Nazarbayev University — whether you are just getting started or are an experienced user looking for advanced technical insights.

At Nazarbayev University, we recognize the essential role HPC plays in modern research, innovation, and problem-solving across disciplines. Our facilities combine advanced technologies with robust infrastructure to support a wide range of demanding computational tasks.

This Wiki is your entry point into the NU HPC ecosystem. Inside, you will find documentation, short tutorials, and best practices on how to make the most of our computing resources. Our goal is to provide the knowledge and support you need to excel in your computational work.

The NU HPC Wiki is built for everyone — students, faculty, and researchers alike. Explore the sections, follow the tutorials, and dive into the topics that matter to you. If you are new to HPC, start with our introductory guide that will help you understand the basics and build confidence step by step.

We also encourage collaboration. Share your expertise, ask questions, and engage with fellow users — the strength of HPC lies in community, and together we can expand the frontiers of computational research and discovery.

Thank you for joining us on this journey. We hope the NU HPC Wiki becomes a valuable resource that empowers you to achieve your goals and drive meaningful innovation.

Happy computing!
